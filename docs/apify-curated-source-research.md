## Selection rules

`curated-sources.js` owns catalog IDs, category routing, API-input adapters, canonical values, FREE-tier event-cost comparison and record normalization. `astraeus-router.js` calls only catalog metadata/build/validation/run/dataset endpoints. No Store search, LLM Actor selection, or LLM-created API input remains on the execution path. AI continues natural-language intake and record-level relevance review.

The candidate order is deterministic: category/geography → schema compatibility → affordable fetched count → lower estimated charge → rating → slug. Unknown/unsupported pricing is not assumed free. These manually approved sources are not filtered out by the obsolete general Store rating cutoff. Sources without native target filters retain explicit `postFilters` in the audit, and record review must require evidence for those criteria. No automatic unrelated-sector widening or paid retry occurs.

Shared included-credit ceiling, per-model caps, reservations, duplicate prevention, HTTPS bearer headers and uncertain-run blocking remain. At most one selected Actor launches per request. Smaller affordable sample sizes can deliver fewer than requested leads. Collection is capped at 200 raw rows; this is deliberately not an unbounded search engine. Metadata can show additional billing events; estimates are not a guaranteed invoice. Apify's per-run charge ceiling is still supplied.

Missing website in a provider record is **not proof that the company has no website**. Wasla labels this uncertainty. Likewise, a usable phone format is not call verification. Catalog presence does not make every target achievable.

## Chat-only government opportunities

Contracting-service intake offers an Etimad card; directly asking about Etimad also exposes it. Nothing is fetched until the user presses **Show tenders**. `POST /lead-agent/tenders` creates an idempotent, account/feature-checked asynchronous search; `GET /lead-agent/tenders/:id` enforces both organization and creating-user scope. Results remain in the chat component, not the Leads workspace. A sample with zero matching open notices is not represented as “no government opportunities exist.”

Each returned notice links only to `https://tenders.etimad.sa/`. General guidance is to open the official notice, use the organization's supplier account, review eligibility/documents/deadlines, purchase the booklet if required, and submit according to that notice. Wasla does not purchase or submit anything. Sources: [Ministry of Finance](https://www.mof.gov.sa/eservices/Pages/Etimad.aspx), [official tender board](https://tenders.etimad.sa/).

## Multi-number display

Numbers are extracted from known contact-key paths, normalized and deduplicated in JavaScript. Primary + `phone_numbers` survive ingestion inside private `raw_payload`. Public APIs disclose extra numbers **only when the lead is revealed**, without exposing the raw dataset or phone-reveal tokens. Compact rows retain their fixed column widths and show a shadcn/Radix `+N` menu; the dossier exposes the same menu. Relay and WhatsApp labels remain distinct.
